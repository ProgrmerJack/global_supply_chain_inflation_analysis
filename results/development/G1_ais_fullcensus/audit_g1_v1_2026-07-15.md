# G1-v1 audit — 15 July 2026

## Scope and immutability

This audit closes G1-v1 as a **failed development validation**. It documents rather than edits `gate_decision_ves_wgt_mo.json`; the companion checksum records its original SHA-256 as `775046fb60e628bdd3e430d96595247137638a33487081cd7aaed00333ced822`.

## What the protocol encoded and what was compared

The historical decision classified the `cargo_tonnage` comparator as `operationally_matched`. The realised pair was AIS `freight_port_calls`—call starts for vessel types 70–89—against imports-only Census vessel shipping weight (`VES_WGT_MO`). It is therefore a **partial construct match**, not a call-for-call operational comparison. The failed relationship does not prove that the NOAA AIS archive is invalid.

All 15 complexes had 120 paired months. The median Pearson correlation was 0.320; 0/15 met 0.80, 4/15 met 0.60, and 5/15 were negative. This is a failed proxy relationship, not a marginal threshold miss and not a partial pass.

## Motion and stationary-state evidence

The registered moving-versus-stationary result was 0.728889 macro-F1 against AIS navigation status. Navigation status is a noisy auxiliary reference within AIS, not an independent gold standard. The result still fails the registered test.

Berth versus anchor is diagnostic only: conditional macro-F1 is 0.990 with **39.146% resolved coverage** (60.854% of stationary observations unresolved). Both quantities must be reported together; this diagnostic cannot support national state-specific duration or emissions claims.

## Governance and consequence

Three historical thresholds remain documented: 0.85 in `config/gates.yml`, 0.80 in the runtime G1 implementation, and a 0.75 state-specific stop in the registered decision tree. The observed motion result is below all three, so the inconsistency does not change G1-v1. The historical configuration and decision are not rewritten.

G1-v1 blocks state-dependent national activity, offshore displacement, emissions, exposure and policy-mechanism claims. It does not permit searching alternative metrics, thresholds, ports or relabellings in the inspected outcomes.

G1-v2 requires a separately preregistered protocol, a definition-led port-specific operational comparator registry, independent call-hour or episode labels with blinded annotation and adjudication, untouched validation evidence, and one canonical threshold source.
