# H6 — 2014-15 labour-disruption replication (independent mechanism check, §8)

**Confirmatory** under `prereg/deep_case_SPB_preregistration.md`. Tests whether the congestion→anchorage
mechanism appears in an independent, **pre-pandemic** shock (the 2014-15 West Coast port labour slowdown) —
different cause, no pandemic demand/fiscal shock. Mode data: pilot `monthly_mode_time_2009_2014.csv` +
`monthly_mode_time.csv` (LA/LB anchor-hours).

## Finding
LA/LB anchor-hours (near-port waiting):
- pre-disruption baseline (2013 H2 – 2014 Q1): **~6,952 /mo**
- disruption peak (2014-11 … 2015-03): **~11,879 /mo (×1.7)**, peaking 2015-03 at **15,063**
- resolved (late 2015): back to ~5,000 /mo

The slowdown (a labour-relations shock) produced the **same physical signature** — anchorage accumulation —
as the 2021 pandemic congestion (which peaked ~25k /mo, larger, but the *mechanism* is identical). The spike
onsets with the slowdown and collapses on its Feb-2015 resolution.

## Verdict (scope corrected per review)
**H6 SUPPORTED — but only for CONGESTION replication, NOT relocation replication.**
- **Supported:** congestion-driven **anchorage accumulation** occurs outside the pandemic (independent cause) —
  validates that the *congestion measurement* generalizes.
- **NOT supported by H6:** that operational interventions **generally redistribute waiting offshore**; that the
  2021 **spatial relocation** mechanism generalizes across policies/gateways. The 2014-15 episode had **no queue
  reform** and this result shows **no near-port→offshore displacement** (no offshore data used). H6 must not be
  cited as external validation of the relocation/emissions mechanism — only of congestion accumulation.

## Caveats
- Magnitude differs (2014-15 ×1.7 vs 2021 much larger) — expected, different shock size.
- The *relocation* test (H1) is not repeated here (no GFW offshore before 2015 at usable resolution); H6
  validates the near-port congestion mechanism, not offshore relocation for 2014-15.
- State-resolved precision inherits the Pillar-B caveat, but the anchor-hours use the validated LA/LB pilot
  classifier.
