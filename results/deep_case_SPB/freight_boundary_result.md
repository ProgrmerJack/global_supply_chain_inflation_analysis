# Official San Pedro Bay freight-sector boundary, 2018–2024

## Result

The official POLA and POLB inventories now form one reproducible five-sector account for ocean-going vessels,
harbor craft, cargo-handling equipment, locomotives and heavy-duty vehicles. All 14 port-year reports were
hash-verified, and all 112 source-category/pollutant totals reproduce the published totals within rounding
tolerance.

The most decision-relevant descriptive contrast is that the climate and local-pollutant boundaries move
differently. Between the separately published 2018 and 2024 inventories:

| Quantity | 2018 SPB total | 2024 SPB total | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CO2e (metric tonnes) | 1,769,262 | 1,799,541 | +1.7% |
| NOx (short tons) | 13,502 | 9,000 | −33.3% |
| DPM (short tons) | 237 | 169 | −28.7% |
| SOx (short tons) | 337 | 255 | −24.3% |

Within CO2e, reported OGV emissions fell 5.0% and cargo-handling emissions fell 7.4%, while heavy-duty-vehicle
emissions rose 14.2%. HDVs consequently increased from 39.9% to 44.8% of the five-sector SPB CO2e account.
For 2024 NOx, OGVs remain the largest reported category at 60.3%; locomotives contribute 16.8% and HDVs 8.3%.

## Evidentiary limits

This is official descriptive boundary accounting, not a causal policy decomposition. Annual inventory editions
can incorporate methodology updates; the cross-edition change fields are therefore context and must not replace
same-methodology restatements when a causal or formal temporal comparison is required. Pollutant mass is reported
in short tons, whereas CO2e is reported in metric tonnes; the pipeline never sums across pollutants or units.

The result does not validate the AIS vessel model, rescue NS-G3, attribute changes to queue or At-Berth policies,
or establish burden transfer between ships and landside sources. It does establish the system boundary that any
future sustainability claim must respect: an OGV-only reduction is not equivalent to a total-freight reduction.

Machine-readable artifacts and checks are in `results/development/spb_freight_boundary/`, generated by
`src/emissions/spb_freight_boundary.py` from the immutable official archive.
